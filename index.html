Approach

Find why it breaks, then fix it.

Through my school projects I've built applications, worked with backend services, debugged issues, and tested functionality. I like finding out why something fails, fixing it properly, and writing down what I learned so the next person doesn't have to guess.

  • 01

    Reproduce before fixing

    I try to get a bug to happen on demand first. A fix I can't verify isn't finished.

  • 02

    Test against requirements

    In LaundryLog I checked valid and invalid input, machine capacity limits, and pricing rules against what the system was supposed to do.

  • 03

    Document honestly

    My READMEs state what each project does, its limits, and exactly which part I built in group work.

  • 04

    Review what AI produces

    I use AI tools while building, then test the result and log where it was wrong. LaundryLog's AI usage log is public.
